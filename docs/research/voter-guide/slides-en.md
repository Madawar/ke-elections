# How elections work: carousel slides

One slide per guide section. Headline 60 characters or fewer. Each body line 120 characters or fewer. The footer line holds the short source.

---

## Slide 1. The six ballots

**Headline:** Six ballots, six colours, six boxes

**Line 1:** President white, Governor light blue, Senator yellow, Woman Rep purple, MP green, MCA beige.

**Line 2:** Each paper goes in the box with the same lid colour.

**Footer:** Source: IEBC notice, 12 Aug 2026

---

## Slide 2. Before the vote

**Headline:** Check your registration at verify.iebc.or.ke

**Line 1:** Party primaries end 8 May 2027. IEBC takes nominations from 29 May to 11 June 2027, by seat.

**Line 2:** Campaigns run 7 am to 6 pm and stop on 7 August 2027.

**Footer:** Source: Gazette Notices 13497–13502, 20 Aug 2026

---

## Slide 3. Election day

**Headline:** Tuesday 10 August 2027: polls open 6 am to 5 pm

**Line 1:** Bring the ID or passport you registered with. In the queue by 5 pm? You still vote.

**Line 2:** Need help? Bring a helper aged 18+ who is not a candidate or agent.

**Footer:** Source: Elections (General) Regulations 2012, regs. 66, 69, 72

---

## Slide 4. How results travel

**Headline:** Every polling station posts its results

**Line 1:** Forms 34A to 39A are signed at the station, and a copy is put up at the door.

**Line 2:** Totals go to the constituency, then county or national tally centres. See forms at forms.iebc.or.ke

**Footer:** Source: Elections (General) Regulations 2012, regs. 79, 83, 87; Elections Act s. 39

---

## Slide 5. After the vote

**Headline:** A result can be challenged in court

**Line 1:** President: Supreme Court petition within 7 days of the declaration. The court decides in 14 days.

**Line 2:** Other seats: petition within 28 days of the declaration.

**Footer:** Source: Constitution of Kenya, Arts. 87(2) and 140

---

## Slide 6. Nominated members

**Headline:** Some seats are filled from party lists

**Line 1:** 12 nominated MPs. 20 nominated Senators: 16 women, 2 youth, 2 people with disabilities.

**Line 2:** Parties get these seats in proportion to the elected seats they win.

**Footer:** Source: Constitution of Kenya, Arts. 90, 97, 98

---

## Slide 7. By-elections

**Headline:** Empty seat in Parliament? By-election within 90 days

**Line 1:** For MP, Woman Rep and Senator seats, no by-election in the 3 months before a general election.

**Line 2:** If a governor leaves office, the deputy governor takes over. No by-election.

**Footer:** Source: Constitution of Kenya, Arts. 101(4)–(5) and 182(2)
