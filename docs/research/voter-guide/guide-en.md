# How elections work

A plain guide to Kenya's next general election on Tuesday 10 August 2027. Every fact has a source. Facts we could not confirm are marked UNVERIFIED.

Facts last checked: 26 November 2026. Laws and dates can change. Always check the latest IEBC notice.

## 1. The six ballots

On election day you get six ballot papers, one for each seat. Each paper has its own colour. You put each paper in the box with the same lid colour.

| Seat | What the job is | Seats | Ballot and lid colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| President | Head of State and Government | 1 | White |
| Governor | Leads the county government | 47 | Light blue |
| Senator | Speaks for the county in the Senate and protects county interests | 47 | Yellow |
| Woman Representative | A Member of the National Assembly elected by the whole county | 47 | Purple |
| Member of the National Assembly (MP) | Represents a constituency, makes national laws, votes on the national budget | 290 | Green |
| Member of County Assembly (MCA) | Represents a ward, makes county laws, oversees the county government | 1,450 | Beige |

IEBC shared these colours on 12 August 2026. Its chart shows colours, not colour names. News reports name some colours differently. Some call the MCA colour "brown". Some call the governor colour "sky blue". One report called the Woman Representative colour "pink".

**Key facts**

- Six seats are on the ballot in every ward. (Constitution Arts. 97, 98, 136, 177, 180)
- There are 290 constituencies. (Constitution Art. 89(1))
- There are 1,450 wards in IEBC's 2027 ward list. (Gazette Notice 13771, 26 August 2026)
- Each ballot paper matches its box lid colour. (IEBC, 12 August 2026)

## 2. Before the vote

**Register.** Any adult Kenyan citizen with a national ID card or valid Kenyan passport can register. You register at one centre only: an IEBC constituency office or a Huduma Centre.

**Check your details.** Visit verify.iebc.or.ke. Enter your ID or passport number and year of birth. News reports say you can also SMS your ID or passport number to 70000. IEBC has not confirmed this for 2027 (UNVERIFIED).

**Parties pick candidates.** A primary is a vote inside a party to choose its candidate. Only members on the party's list can vote in it. Parties must send IEBC certified membership lists by 12 April 2027. Primaries and party disputes must end by 8 May 2027.

**IEBC nominations.** Candidates hand their papers to IEBC returning officers. IEBC checks that each candidate qualifies, then clears them. Each seat has its own dates. Complaints about IEBC nominations must be filed by 12 June and decided by 23 June 2027.

**Campaigns.** Campaigns run from 7 am to 6 pm only. They start between 29 May and 7 June, by seat. All campaigns stop on Saturday 7 August 2027.

**Key facts**

- Election day: Tuesday 10 August 2027. (2027 timetable)
- IEBC nomination dates: President 29 May to 11 June; MP 29 May to 1 June; Senator 29 May to 2 June; MCA 2 to 11 June; Governor 3 to 6 June; Woman Representative 7 to 11 June 2027. (Gazette Notices 13497 to 13502)
- Party lists for nominated seats are due by Friday 25 June 2027. (Gazette Notices 13498 to 13502)
- Other deadlines: no fundraising or harambee by aspirants after 9 December 2026; public officers who want to run must resign by 10 February 2027; independent candidates must leave their parties by 8 May 2027. (2027 timetable)

**Sources**

- Constitution Art. 83 (who can register), Art. 85 (independent candidates), Art. 137 (presidential nomination needs 2,000 voters from each of a majority of counties): https://www.parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-03/The_Constitution_of_Kenya_2010.pdf

## 3. Election day

Polling stations open at 6 am and close at 5 pm. If you are in the queue by 5 pm, you still vote.

1. Bring the ID card or passport you registered with.
2. A clerk checks your fingerprint on an electronic kit (the KIEMS kit). If it fails, officials find you in the printed register while agents watch.
3. You get all six ballot papers at once.
4. In a private booth, mark each paper with a tick, cross or thumbprint next to your choice. Fold it.
5. Put each paper in the box with the matching lid colour. Your finger is inked, then you leave.

**Need help?** If you have a disability or cannot read or write, you may bring a helper aged 18 or over. The helper cannot be a candidate or agent. Or the presiding officer helps you while agents watch.

**Who watches.** Each candidate or party may have one agent inside to watch for them. Accredited observers and media may also watch.

**Counting.** After closing, votes are counted at the station, in this order: President, MP, MCA, Senator, Woman Representative, Governor.

**Key facts**

- Voting hours: 6 am to 5 pm; people in the queue at 5 pm still vote. (Regulations reg. 66)
- Fingerprint check; printed register as backup. (Regulations reg. 69; Elections Act ss. 44, 44A)
- One agent per candidate or party inside the station. (Regulations reg. 62(2))
- No more than 700 voters per polling station. (Elections Act s. 38A)

**Sources**

- Regulations regs. 62, 66, 69, 70, 72, 75, 76: https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/902SFZiDqt.pdf
- Elections Act ss. 30, 38A, 44, 44A: https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/G16qxyy4rA.pdf

## 4. How results travel

**At the polling station.** The presiding officer fills one results form for each seat. Candidates or agents sign it. Agents get copies. A copy is put up at the polling station entrance for anyone to read.

**Sending results.** The presiding officer sends the results electronically. Then the paper forms go to the constituency tallying centre. For President, the law says results must be sent both electronically and on paper. IEBC must also put the polling station forms on a public website.

**At the constituency.** The returning officer adds up all polling stations. The MP and MCA winners are declared here. Governor, Senator and Woman Representative totals go to the county tallying centre. President totals go to the national tallying centre.

**Look up a form yourself.** Open forms.iebc.or.ke. Choose the election, the seat and the area. Download the scanned form. Today the site holds forms from 2022 and from by-elections since then.

**Key facts**

- Polling station results are announced, signed and posted at the station. (Regulations reg. 79)
- Results are sent electronically before paper forms leave the station. (Regulations reg. 82)
- For President, IEBC must publish polling station forms on a public online portal. (Elections Act s. 39(1C))
- If electronic and paper results differ, the result declared at the polling station prevails. (Elections Act s. 39(1E))

## 5. After the vote

**Who declares the winner.**

- MP and MCA: the constituency returning officer.
- Governor, Senator and Woman Representative: the county returning officer.
- President: the IEBC chairperson, within 7 days after the election.

A presidential winner needs more than half of all votes cast, and at least 25% of votes in more than half of the counties. IEBC then lists the winners in the Kenya Gazette, the government's official notice paper.

**Going to court.** A petition is a court case that challenges a result.

- President: file at the Supreme Court within 7 days of the declaration. The court decides within 14 days.
- Other seats: file within 28 days after IEBC declares the result. MP, Senator, Woman Representative and Governor cases go to the High Court. MCA cases go to a magistrate's court chosen by the Chief Justice. These courts decide within 6 months.

**Taking office.** With no petition, the new President is sworn in on the first Tuesday after the 14th day following the declaration. If the Supreme Court upholds the win, it is the first Tuesday after the 7th day following the ruling.

**Key facts**

- President declared within 7 days of the election. (Constitution Art. 138(10))
- Presidential petition: 7 days to file, 14 days to decide. (Constitution Art. 140)
- Other petitions: within 28 days of the declaration (not of gazettement). (Constitution Art. 87(2); Elections Act s. 76(1)(a))
- Governors are sworn in on the first Thursday after the 10th day following the declaration, if there is no petition. (Assumption of the Office of Governor Act, 2019; UNVERIFIED against the Act's text)

**Sources**

- Constitution Arts. 87, 105, 138, 140, 141: https://www.parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-03/The_Constitution_of_Kenya_2010.pdf
- Elections Act ss. 39, 75, 76, 85A: https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/G16qxyy4rA.pdf

## 6. Nominated members

Some seats are not on the ballot. Parties fill them from lists they give IEBC before the election. For 2027, these party lists are due by 25 June 2027.

- **National Assembly:** 12 nominated members. They represent special interests such as youth, people with disabilities and workers.
- **Senate:** 16 women, 2 youth and 2 people with disabilities. That is 20 in total. Each youth pair and disability pair is one man and one woman.
- **County assemblies:** "special seat" members are added so that no more than two-thirds of members are of one gender. More members represent marginalised groups, people with disabilities and youth.

**How seats are shared.** Each party gets nominated seats in proportion to the elected seats it won. In the National Assembly, this counts MPs and Woman Representatives. In the Senate, it counts elected Senators. In a county assembly, it counts wards won in that county. Most party lists must alternate between men and women. IEBC names the nominated members within 30 days after results are declared.

**Key facts**

- 12 nominated MPs. (Constitution Art. 97(1)(c))
- 20 nominated Senators: 16 women + 2 youth + 2 people with disabilities. (Constitution Art. 98(1)(b) to (d))
- County assembly gender top-up: no more than two-thirds of one gender. (Constitution Art. 177(1)(b))
- Marginalised-group seats per county assembly: 4 under the Elections Act. Another law, the County Governments Act, is reported to say 6. (Elections Act s. 36(8); the figure of 6 is UNVERIFIED)

**Sources**

- Constitution Arts. 90, 97, 98, 177: https://www.parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-03/The_Constitution_of_Kenya_2010.pdf
- Elections Act ss. 34, 35, 36, 37: https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/G16qxyy4rA.pdf

## 7. By-elections

A by-election fills an elected seat that becomes empty between general elections. A seat can become empty if the holder dies, resigns, is removed, or misses 8 sittings without permission.

**MP, Woman Representative and Senator.** The Speaker tells IEBC within 21 days. IEBC must hold a by-election within 90 days. But the Constitution bans filling these seats in the 3 months just before a general election. By our count, for 2027 that ban starts on 10 May 2027.

**Governor.** There is no by-election. The deputy governor takes over for the rest of the term. If both offices are empty, an election must happen within 60 days.

**President.** The Deputy President takes over for the rest of the term.

**MCA.** The county assembly speaker tells IEBC within 21 days. IEBC then sets a date by Gazette notice. The Elections Act gives no fixed deadline for ward by-elections. Example: Migwani ward (Kitui) fell vacant on 6 August 2026. IEBC set its by-election for 17 November 2026.

**Nominated seats.** There is no by-election. The next person of the same gender on the party list takes the seat.

**Key facts**

- By-election within 90 days of the vacancy. (Constitution Art. 101(4))
- No MP, Woman Representative or Senator by-election in the last 3 months before a general election. (Constitution Art. 101(5))
- Governor vacancy: deputy governor takes over. (Constitution Art. 182(2))
- Whether an MCA seat that falls vacant close to 10 August 2027 will be filled: UNVERIFIED. No rule found; check the IEBC notice for that ward.

**Sources**

- Constitution Arts. 101, 103, 146, 182, 194: https://www.parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-03/The_Constitution_of_Kenya_2010.pdf
- Elections Act ss. 16, 19, 37: https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/G16qxyy4rA.pdf
- Gazette Notice 15266 (Migwani ward by-election): https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/ldzLYApcT4.pdf

## How to use this app with the guide

Start on the map and find your ward. Your ward sits inside a constituency, and your constituency sits inside a county. Together they tell you which six seats you vote for. Tap your area to see who represents you today. If 2027 candidate or results data is added to the app later, use the form numbers in section 4 to find the matching IEBC form at forms.iebc.or.ke. You can then check the numbers yourself.
